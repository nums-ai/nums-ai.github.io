## Building a foundation for structured data {#building-a-foundation-for-structured-data}

Nums AI has raised ₩4 billion in its first funding round. Stonebridge Ventures led the round, joined by SBVA, KT Investment, and BASS Ventures.

We are building tabular foundation models for tables and numbers. Our work connects model research, pretraining, inference systems, and real customer problems. Causilo is our foundation model for classification and regression.

## Join the team {#join-the-team}

We are expanding our team in Seoul. Our Careers page introduces the work, current positions in machine learning engineering and business development, and the opportunity to propose a new role through an open application.
