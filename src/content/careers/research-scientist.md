You’ll advance the research behind our tabular foundation models. You’ll develop ideas for how models learn from structured data, design experiments to understand why they work, and build stronger pretrained models.

## What you’ll do

- Develop model architectures, training objectives, and in-context learning methods for structured data.
- Design synthetic data generation methods and pretraining tasks that help models generalize to real datasets.
- Evaluate predictive quality, robustness, and efficiency across diverse benchmarks.

## What we’re looking for

- Experience turning a research idea into a working implementation and evaluating it against meaningful baselines.
- Strong Python skills and experience implementing and training models with a deep learning framework such as PyTorch.
- A solid understanding of deep learning, probability, statistics, and experimental design.
- The ability to read research papers, formulate hypotheses, and design effective experiments.

## Nice to have

- Experience researching tabular foundation models, including architectures that model interactions across rows and columns.
- Experience generating tabular data using structural causal models, Bayesian networks, or probabilistic programs.
- Experience running pretraining across multiple GPUs over days or weeks.
- Experience evaluating tabular models under distribution shift, missing data, or changes in feature types.
