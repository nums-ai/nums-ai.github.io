You’ll build systems that serve our tabular foundation models (TFMs) efficiently. You’ll develop inference infrastructure for diverse datasets and deployment environments, and turn customer problems into improvements to our products and research.

## What you’ll do

- Design and improve inference pipelines for pretrained TFMs.
- Extend TFMs to support a wider range of data and query types.
- Deploy models through APIs, on-premises installations, and other formats suited to each customer environment.
- Evaluate predictive quality, inference speed, scalability, reliability, and usability.
- Investigate recurring customer needs and failure cases, and use the findings to improve product capabilities.

## What we’re looking for

- Experience developing models with a deep learning framework such as PyTorch.
- Strong Python skills and the ability to write testable, maintainable code.
- A solid understanding of machine learning training, inference pipelines, and data processing.
- The ability to analyze experiments systematically and produce reproducible results.
- The initiative to turn ambiguous problems into clear goals and follow through on a solution.

## Nice to have

- Experience with transformers, tabular models, graph neural networks, or in-context learning.
- Experience with distributed training, GPU profiling, or inference optimization.
- Experience building and operating model-serving systems, APIs, or data pipelines.
- Experience deploying machine learning systems in cloud or on-premises environments.
- Experience applying recent research to real-world machine learning systems.
