You’ll help define the role Nums AI needs next. You’ll identify an important problem we may not have considered and propose how you would help solve it.

## What we’re looking for

We welcome proposals across roles and fields. There are no fixed requirements for title, experience, or field of study.

## What to include

- Describe the role you believe Nums AI needs next.
- Explain the problem you would solve and the outcome you would aim to achieve.
- Share the experience or body of work that shows why you would succeed in this role.
- Outline what you would aim to accomplish in your first three months.
